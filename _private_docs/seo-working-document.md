## FAQ Bank

These can be reused across landing pages, but each page should use only the most relevant questions.

### Cost And Pricing

Question: How much does a small business website cost in South Africa?

Draft answer: Intellify offers managed website subscriptions from R150 per month with no large upfront build cost. The monthly plan includes the website build, hosting, domain registration, annual domain renewal, and ongoing technical support, depending on the selected package.

Question: Why use a monthly website subscription instead of paying once off?

Draft answer: A monthly website subscription spreads the cost of getting online and keeps the technical work managed after launch. Instead of paying a large build fee and then separately arranging hosting, renewals, security, and updates, everything is handled through one predictable monthly plan.

Question: Are hosting and domain costs included?

Draft answer: Yes. Intellify plans include managed hosting plus domain registration and annual renewal while the subscription remains active, subject to the plan terms.

### Package Choice

Question: Which website package is right for a new small business?

Draft answer: Starter is often enough for a focused first presence. Recommended is better when the business needs multiple pages, a contact form, stronger service detail, and regular monthly changes. Premium is suited to businesses that want richer media, analytics, and more frequent improvements.

Question: Can I upgrade my website plan later?

Draft answer: Yes. The service is designed so your website can grow over time. You can start with a focused website and add more pages, sections, forms, video, analytics, or content support when the business needs them.

### SEO And Performance

Question: Are Intellify websites built with SEO in mind?

Draft answer: Yes. Intellify websites are built to be mobile-friendly, crawlable, structured around clear services and locations, and technically ready for Google. Premium plans include additional SEO support and analytics.

Question: Will my website rank on Google?

Draft answer: Intellify builds the technical and content foundation that helps Google understand your website, but rankings depend on competition, content quality, backlinks, search demand, and ongoing improvement. No ethical provider should guarantee a specific ranking.

### Process

Question: How long does it take to launch a website?

Draft answer: Most small business websites can be planned, built, and launched within two to three weeks once the core content is ready. The exact timeline depends on the number of pages, features, images, copy, and approval speed.

Question: What do I need to provide before you build my website?

Draft answer: Helpful starting material includes your business name, services, contact details, logo if available, preferred domain, photos or brand images, and a short explanation of what visitors should understand or do on the site.

### Support

Question: Do you make updates after launch?

Draft answer: Yes. Ongoing support and technical management are included. Some plans include monthly content changes, with higher plans allowing more frequent changes.

Question: Can Intellify redesign an existing website?

Draft answer: Yes. Intellify can review an existing website and recommend whether a focused redesign, a fresh managed build, or a phased improvement plan makes the most sense.
